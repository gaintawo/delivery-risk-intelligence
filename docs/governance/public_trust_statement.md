# Public Trust Statement

*For customers and sellers. Written to be read in two minutes.*

**What it is.** Delivery Risk Intelligence is a tool that estimates, when you place an order, how likely it is to arrive after the date we promised. Its purpose is simple: to spot problems early so we can fix them, or tell you honestly before you have to ask.

**What it uses.** Facts about the order that are known when it is placed: how many days were promised, how far the parcel travels, the season, and how the seller has delivered before. It does not use your name, address, postcode, contact details or review text. Location is used only at the level of your state.

**What it does not do.** It does not make decisions on its own. A person always decides what to do. It never raises prices, cancels orders or penalises a seller. A flag on an order means "give this order extra care", nothing more.

**How good it is.** It is right about which orders are riskier about 7 times in 10. It still misses most late orders, and most flagged orders arrive on time. We say this openly so nobody over-trusts it.

**Fairness.** We test whether late orders are caught equally well in every region of Brazil. Today they are not, so the tool is **not in use**: it runs in the background only, and nobody acts on its scores. It will not be used until the gap between regions is 10 points or less and an independent Review Board, which includes a customer and a seller representative, approves it.

**Your rights.** Every score is stored with its reasons. If a decision ever affects you, you can ask why and ask a person to review it. Contact the Data Protection Officer through the marketplace's privacy page. We will reply within 15 days.

**Our commitment.** If the tool causes harm or stops working fairly, we will pause it, tell the people affected, fix it or retire it, and publish what we learned.
